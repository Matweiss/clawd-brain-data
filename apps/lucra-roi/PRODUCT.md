# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Lucra sales representatives use this calculator live during discovery and commercial calls to shape deals, test assumptions, compare economics, and generate approved customer materials. Prospect-facing outputs are secondary and must remain clearly separated from internal sales guidance and assumptions.

## Product Purpose

The Lucra ROI Calculator turns deal inputs into trustworthy financial scenarios and agreement-ready outputs across Lucra's core commercial workflows. Success means a rep can start from a credible deal archetype, reach a defensible ROI view quickly, explain the assumptions clearly, and generate the appropriate customer deliverable without navigating a wall of peer tabs or risking calculation drift.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, competitive, trustworthy. The product should feel commercially confident and energetic without becoming noisy, theatrical, or gambling-forward. Copy is direct, specific, and grounded in measurable outcomes.

## Anti-references

- A generic SaaS dashboard made from repeated identical cards.
- A casino, sportsbook, or luck-forward visual language.
- Decorative neon, glassmorphism, gradients used as filler, or dark-mode glow everywhere.
- A spreadsheet-like wall of inputs with no guided sequence or clear decision hierarchy.
- A prospect-facing experience that exposes internal coaching, unsupported claims, or unlabelled assumptions.

## Design Principles

1. Start with the deal, not the calculator. Guide the rep from archetype and discovery inputs to results and deliverables.
2. Make trust visible. Label assumptions, sources, scenario boundaries, and estimate status at the point of use.
3. Preserve calculation truth. Presentation may change; the tested formulas and agreement workflow must not drift.
4. Use brand energy for action and state. Lucra Play green marks selection, progress, and primary action rather than decorating every surface.
5. Keep prospect-safe output deliberate. Internal guidance and customer-ready content are separate modes, never accidental mixtures.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, semantics, keyboard interaction, focus visibility, error identification, touch targets, and reduced motion. The full workflow must remain usable at 200% text zoom and at 320px, 375px, 768px, 1024px, and wide desktop widths.
